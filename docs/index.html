THE IDEA

Scattered across too many places

Credit cards bury liabilities in statement cycles. Bank accounts scatter cash across three different apps. You open multiple banking portals just to figure out how much you can actually afford to spend this weekend. Personal finance shouldn't feel like auditing an enterprise or handing your financial life over to cloud advertisers.

WHAT YOU GET

Six things worth the install

01 [OFFLINE-FIRST]

Your data never leaves the phone

No remote server, no cloud accounts, no analytics SDKs. Everything is persisted locally in SQLite via Room using exact integer minor units (paise).

02 [CREDIT CARDS]

True credit card accounting

Cards feature dedicated charcoal banners, outstanding liability balances, and available credit headroom. Bill settlements reduce debt without distorting expense totals.

03 [TIME PICKER]

Custom 12-hour stepper sheet

A bottom sheet built for thumbs. Instant hour and minute steppers with wrapping, AM/PM segmented toggle, and 50/50 confirmation actions.

04 [TEMPLATES & TYPES]

Context-aware fast entry

Templates adapt strictly to the transaction type — only expense templates when logging an expense, only income templates for earnings. Never misfile.

05 [CATEGORIES & PASTELS]

Quiet monochrome with soft accents

Subtle pastel circular badges distinguish categories at a glance while preserving Expend's timeless black-and-white visual identity.

06 [BACKUPS & PORTABILITY]

Versioned JSON backups (v5)

Export or restore your complete financial ledger in human-readable, schema-versioned JSON. Backward-compatible with all earlier versions.

HOW IT'S BUILT

For the ones who'll ask what's underneath

A modern, production-grade Android stack: Kotlin 2.0+, Jetpack Compose Material 3, Room SQLite with automated schema migrations, Kotlin Coroutines & StateFlow, and 25 passing unit tests.

ARCHITECTURE

Deterministic data flow with integer minor units

Transactions enter via dedicated forms or templates, flow through the financial engine where currency is computed in integer paise, persist into Room SQLite (v5 schema), and reactively project across Compose UI states.
